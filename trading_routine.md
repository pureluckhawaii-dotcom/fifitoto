# Trading Routine

## Pre-Market Preparation
- Review overnight news and market developments
- Check economic calendar for scheduled announcements
- Analyze pre-market movers and gaps
- Review watchlist and update as needed

## Market Open (First 30 Minutes)
- Observe price action without trading
- Identify key support and resistance levels
- Note volume and volatility patterns
- Wait for clear setups before entering

## Active Trading Session
- Follow your trade plan and entry criteria
- Manage position sizes according to risk rules
- Set stop-losses before entering any trade
- Avoid chasing trades or revenge trading

## Position Management
- Monitor open positions regularly
- Adjust stops as trades move in your favor
- Take partial profits at key levels
- Do not move stops against your position

## End of Day Review
- Log all trades with entry, exit, and rationale
- Calculate daily P&L
- Review what went well and what to improve
- Update watchlist for the next session

## Risk Management Rules
- Never risk more than 1-2% of capital per trade
- Maximum 3 losing trades before stopping for the day
- Do not trade during high-impact news events unless planned
- Keep a trading journal to track performance over time
